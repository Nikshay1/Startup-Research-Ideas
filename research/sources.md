## YC Requests for Startups, Fall 2026

YC's own list of what it wants to fund (https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs):

1. **The Primer:** an app that teaches young children reading, writing and maths as well as a private tutor.
2. **American defense:** low-cost interceptors, sensors, drones and their manufacturing.
3. **A cloud for small software:** hosting for one-off apps, as easy to share as a Google Doc.
4. **Multiplayer AI:** AI agent sessions a whole team can watch, redirect and hand over.
5. **Compute at sea:** data centers on ships.
6. **Consumer AI for 1 billion people:** everyday tasks such as getting around, learning, health, money.
7. **AI for the ageing population:** voice interfaces, safety monitoring, home robots, caregiver coordination.
8. **Operating systems for the physical world:** software that manages mixed teams of AI agents, robots and people in construction, maintenance and fleets.
9. **Crypto:** stablecoins, AI-agent commerce, trading, institutional products.
10. **Data for the real world:** dense physical-world data for energy, farming, logistics, weather.
11. **Proving you are human:** a privacy-safe way to verify a real person is on a call or transaction.
12. **AI-native compliance:** software that watches regulations and keeps audit trails across countries.
13. **Self-maintaining APIs:** agents that fix customer code when an API provider changes something.
